# Health Records Summary Merge Guidance

When merging multiple summaries of health record sections, follow these guidelines:

## Merge Priorities

1. **Preserve all unique clinical information** - Do not drop findings that appear in only one section
2. **Consolidate duplicates** - Combine repeated information into single entries with the most complete/recent data
3. **Maintain chronology** - Keep temporal relationships clear when merging events from different sections
4. **Resolve conflicts** - When summaries contain conflicting information, preserve both with clear notation

## Structural Approach

- Group by clinical category (diagnoses, medications, labs, procedures, etc.)
- Within categories, order chronologically or by clinical priority
- Cross-reference related findings (e.g., link abnormal lab to diagnosis)
- Preserve quantitative trends rather than isolated values when available

## What to Combine

- Multiple mentions of the same diagnosis → single entry with full context
- Lab values from same panel → consolidated with date
- Medication mentions → current list with relevant history
- Repeat procedures → summarized with key findings from each

## What to Keep Separate

- Distinct clinical events (different hospitalizations, procedures)
- Evolving findings that show progression
- Different clinical opinions or interpretations
- Time-sensitive findings with specific dates
